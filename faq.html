Common questions

FAQ

Short answers to the things people ask before running the analysis.

Is it safe to log in?+
Login always happens manually by you, inside an embedded browser panel. The bot never reads or stores your credentials, and it doesn't keep them anywhere on disk.
Do you save my data?+
No. Only the usernames collected during analysis are returned to your browser session to build the comparison; nothing is persisted on any server.
Why does it ask me to log in manually?+
So that Instagram sees a normal, human login from your own session. Automating the login itself would be much more likely to trigger security flags on the account.
Is scraping against Instagram's rules?+
Automated access and scraping of Instagram violate their Terms of Service and can lead to account restrictions or bans. This tool is for personal and educational use on your own account — use it at low frequency and at your own risk.
What account should I use?+
Preferably a secondary account. It limits any risk to your main profile, and the tool works the same on any account you own.
Can I export the results?+
Yes. After an analysis, use the filter box to refine the list, copy it to your clipboard, or download the whole "not following back" list as a CSV.
