Privacy

Overview

InstaFollowCheck runs locally on your machine. Its design goal is to keep as little data flowing through the tool as possible.

What we do with your data

Login credentials are typed by you into an embedded browser panel and are never read or stored by the tool. During analysis, only public usernames are collected to build the followers / following comparison — and those results only exist in your browser session.

Local processing

The analysis happens in a background browser on your device. No account information, list of usernames, or results are sent to or stored on any external server.

Cookies & sessions

The tool uses a local browsing profile to keep your Instagram session. You can log out at any time from the tool, which clears that session's cookies.

Third parties

Instagram and Meta are separate companies. This is an independent, unofficial tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Meta / Instagram.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the tool evolves. You can always review the latest version on this page.
