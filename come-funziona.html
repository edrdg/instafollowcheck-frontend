The process

How it
works

Three steps, fully under your control. The bot scrolls in the background; you stay in the driver's seat for login.

01

Open the tool

Head to the Analyzer and press Login. An embedded browser view opens right in the page — no separate window pops up, because the bot runs headless in the background.

Go to /strumento
02

Log in manually

Type your username and password directly inside the embedded view. InstaFollowCheck never reads or stores your credentials — the login is 100% yours.

You type · We never store
03

Analyze

Press Analyze account. The bot opens your profile, scrolls the followers and following lists, and compares both sets into one clean result.

Scroll → Compare → Export
